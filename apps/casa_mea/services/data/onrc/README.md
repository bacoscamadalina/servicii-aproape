# ONRC source bundle

`manifest-2026-07-08.json` fixes the official ONRC snapshot selected for the first
catalogue audit. The three business exports total about 1.21 GB and are deliberately
not committed to the repository. Downloaded files must be kept outside version control,
checksummed locally, audited together, and attributed to ONRC under CC BY 4.0.

The legal office locality is not a service coverage area. Raw street addresses,
representatives, phone numbers, and email addresses are not part of the public ServiciiAproape
listing pipeline.
