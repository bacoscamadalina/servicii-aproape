# Provider access quality bar

Seed key: `incumbent-serviciiaproape-toolmaker-catalogue-v1`

This surface extends the approved Professional Toolmaker Catalogue documented in
`DESIGN.md`; it does not introduce a replacement visual world or a decision comp.

- Form: Operate, code-led, form-first on mobile.
- Type: Nimbus Narrow for operational headings and actions; Carlito for instructions.
- Material: mineral paper, square blue-steel instruments, one ambient instrument shadow.
- Signal: ochre is reserved for the current primary action; green reports saved state.
- Truth: administrative approval, account activation, draft editing and publication
  remain visibly separate. ONRC facts are never presented as editable.
- Finish: no horizontal overflow at 391 CSS px; complete task and submit action in the
  first mobile viewport; explicit focus, error, expired, saved and logged-out states.
